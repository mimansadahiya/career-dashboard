Own money changes decisions. A recall is a required safety fix Ford pays for. A tariff is an import tax Ford pays. A write-off cuts reported profit. All three problems follow records Ford already has: repairs, vehicle data, supplier updates, real cost per vehicle. The fix is speed, not new factories.

Problem 01

Recalls

152
In 2025 · Most ever

152 recalls in 2025 — the most of any carmaker in one year (old record: 77). About 40 were repeats: software fixes sent but never confirmed installed. Source: CBT News

Do now

Freeze launches that fail quality gates.

No ship-date override, ever. Late trucks beat recalled trucks.

This quarter

Pay dealers to report defects first.

$500 for a new failure pattern, paid on proof. Cheapest early warning Ford can buy.

Big call

Bill suppliers for defect costs.

Put warranty recovery in every contract. Quality becomes their money problem too.

Crazy

Halve the options list.

Fewer trims, fewer parts, fewer ways to fail. Complexity is the defect factory.

Problem 02

Supply shocks

~1 week
F-150 plants idle · Sept 2026

One missing part idled the F-150 plants for about a week (Dearborn, Kansas City). Aluminum supplier fires add up to ~$1.5B, ~$800M in early 2025 alone. Sources: Yahoo Finance · Detroit Free Press

Do now

Stockpile the 20 parts that stop lines.

Cash on shelves is cheaper than a stopped plant.

This quarter

Ban single sources on critical parts.

Two suppliers or the CFO signs a written exception. No silent risks.

Big call

Prepay for capacity — or buy a stake.

Money upfront buys priority when everyone is short.

Crazy

Own the bottleneck: fund a second aluminum plant.

If one fire can stop the F-150, Ford should own the backup.

Problem 03

EV losses

$4.8B
Lost on EVs in 2025

$4.8B lost on EVs in 2025; $19.5B written off. $4–4.5B more losses expected in 2026; break-even only by 2029. Tariffs cost ~$2B. Source: NY Post / Fox Business

Do now

Kill any model that can't show profit in 12 months.

No exceptions for strategy slides. Lightning was already cut — finish the job.

This quarter

Stop building platforms. Partner instead.

License or co-build the EV base. Keep Ford's money for hybrids and trucks that earn today.

Big call

Pay EV leaders per vehicle, not per promise.

Bonuses tied to profit per unit. What gets paid gets done.

Crazy

Hive off the EV unit to a partner.

Hand the losses to someone with cheaper capital. Keep software, Pro and the brand.
