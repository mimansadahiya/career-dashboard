PROBLEM 01

Quality & recalls

152 recallsIN 2025 · INDUSTRY RECORD

An industry record - and roughly 40 were reissued software fixes never verified as installed. Ford doubled its safety team and warranty costs are easing, but field failures still jam dealer service bays and strand new vehicles. CBT News

Quick win · ≤90 days

One quality scoreboard

Warranty claims, repair notes and vehicle alerts on a single monthly page, red items owned by name.

Effect: same facts for everyone; weeks not quarters.
Watch: dealer notes lag and vary.
Core · 3-9 months

Defect smoke alarms

Alerts when failures cluster by batch, plant or model; every software fix verified as installed.

Effect: catch defects before regulators; end repeat recalls.
Watch: false alarms erode trust.
Bold · 9-24 months

Vehicle health score

A 1-100 score per vehicle; owners and fleets fix issues before failure. Opt-in.

Effect: fewer breakdowns, stronger service loyalty.
Watch: privacy optics; pilot first.
Moonshot · 24+ months

Zero-recall loop

Fleet digital twin tests every change pre-production; bounties pay for early defect reports.

Effect: quality becomes a brand premium again.
Watch: culture, supplier buy-in, legal.

PROBLEM 02

Supply chain shocks

~1 weekF-150 LINE HALT · SEPT 2026

One supplier stopped the profit engine: Dearborn and Kansas City idled; the Novelis aluminum fires cost ~$800M in the first half of 2025 alone, ~$1.5B expected in total. Yahoo Finance · Detroit Free Press

Quick win · ≤90 days

Supplier weather map

Top 200 parts scored for single-source risk, financial health, hazard exposure and stock on hand.

Effect: the top 20 stop-the-line risks, visible.
Watch: visibility stops at tier one.
Core · 3-9 months

Control tower + drills

Live supplier, port and weather signals with playbooks; rehearse the top-20 losses quarterly.

Effect: downtime days become hours; premium freight falls.
Watch: alert fatigue; start at top 50 parts.
Bold · 9-24 months

Buffers as insurance

Pay a deliberate premium - stock or second source - where expected loss justifies it.

Effect: small carrying cost, avoided nine-figure shocks.
Watch: capital trade-offs; labor constraints.
Moonshot · 24+ months

Shock insurance pool

Price every critical part like an insurer, fund buffers and micro-manufacturing fallbacks.

Effect: on-time trucks in bad years - a selling point.
Watch: complexity; supplier trust.

PROBLEM 03

EV losses & tariffs

$11.1BQ4 2025 NET LOSS · LARGEST SINCE 2008

The capital-allocation pivot: $4.8B lost on EVs in 2025, a $19.5B write-down, break-even only by 2029 - plus ~$2B in tariffs expected again. NY Post / Fox Business

Quick win · ≤90 days

Profit per vehicle

One monthly scorecard per model and plant: price minus material, tariff, battery, warranty, incentives.

Effect: keep / fix / kill decisions on facts.
Watch: cost data lives in many systems.
Core · 3-9 months

Scenario room

Rolling what-ifs on tariffs, credits, demand and gas prices - with pre-agreed plant-level decision gates.

Effect: the response is ready before the news.
Watch: assumptions must be finance-owned.
Bold · 9-24 months

Hybrid-first + software margin

Fund where margin is today; cap EV burn; grow subscriptions and fleet services as recurring income.

Effect: less dependence on one-time metal margins.
Watch: capability gaps; credible pricing.
Moonshot · 24+ months

Energy & uptime company

Vehicle-to-grid income, battery second life, and uptime-as-a-service for commercial fleets.

Effect: recurring revenue; hedge against EV volume.
Watch: utility regulation; upfront capital.
