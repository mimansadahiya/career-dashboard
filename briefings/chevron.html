Chevron's record quarter rests on war-driven oil prices, yet every Kazakh barrel still exits through one exposed artery: Tengiz to the Black Sea. The fix is redundancy in power, routes and people, not deeper cuts that hollow out the operators keeping barrels moving.CPC (Caspian Pipeline Consortium) is the pipeline system that carries Kazakh crude to a Black Sea terminal. When drones hit tankers there, Tengiz, Chevron's 50%-owned supergiant field, cannot sell what it pumps.

Do now

Insure every CPC tanker or stop sailing

This quarter

Push Kazakh barrels east to China

Use the existing pipeline before the Black Sea closes.

Big call

Fund private drone defense for the terminal

If the sea is unsafe, pay to make it safe.

Crazy

Problem 03 · Financial Strategy

Chevron is cutting up to 9,000 jobs after its best quarter in six years

$2-3B
cost cuts targeted by end of 2026

The 15-20% cut means more than 9,000 layoffs on 2023 headcount; Q2 2026 adjusted earnings hit $12.0B. Sources: CNBC: Chevron slashes up to 20% of workforce · Reuters: Chevron to lay off up to 20% of global workforce · Chevron investors: 2Q 2026 results and metrics · Reuters: highest quarterly profit in six years

Do now

Freeze the layoff list, cut contractors first

Protect the operators who keep barrels flowing.

This quarter

Reinvest savings into AI and data centers

Buy efficiency with machines, not only with exits.

Big call

Spin off the fuel retail business

A $248M refining loss does not belong inside an oil major.

Crazy

Retrain every laid-off worker as a power technician

Turn 9,000 exits into 9,000 scarce skills.
