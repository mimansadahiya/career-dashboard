BNSF's trains are fuller but its profit per haul is shrinking: costs grew faster than revenue, a rival merger could redraw the map, and coal keeps fading.Operating ratio means cost as a share of revenue — lower is better. The fix starts with per-route cost and demand forecasts, not new locomotives.

Problem 01 · Costs

Revenue rose 15% but costs rose 16% — profit per haul shrank

68%
Q2 2026 fuel cost jump

Volume grew 7% to 2.5M units and the operating ratio still worsened 0.6 points; fuel hit $1.2B and pay $1.4B. Sources: Progressive Railroading, Q2 2026 · BNSF Q2 2026 summary (PDF)

Problem 03 · Coal

Coal volume fell 8% — the only shrinking business

−8%
Coal units, Q2 2026

Retirements and cheap gas drove the drop; coal revenue still rose 1% to $722M on price, while intermodal grew 9%. Sources: Progressive Railroading, Q2 2026

Do now

Date every coal plant's closure

Treat shutdowns like churn — plant by plant.

This quarter

Grow with the 9% winner.

Big call

Get paid when plants run less.

Crazy

Build rail links for AI data centers

New heavy freight: power, fuel, equipment.
