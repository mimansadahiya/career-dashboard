Problem 01 · LNG

A war knocked out 17% of Qatar's LNG — Shell trades the swings

65%
projected LNG demand growth by 2050

Q2 2026 profit more than doubled to $9.84B on trading and refining; Strait of Hormuz recovery is still uncertain. Sources: LNG Prime · Reuters energy coverage

Do now

Rank every cargo by margin-at-risk, daily

Know the best reroute before sailing.

This quarter

Buy flexible landing slots, not fixed routes

Options beat schedules in a shock.

Big call

Take more LNG Canada volume while rivals pause

Cheap when the strait is shut.

Crazy

Buy floating storage to bank the glut

Sell into the next spike.

Problem 02 · Chemicals

Chemicals lost money — the $3.5B buyback wobbled

$24–26B
2026 capex plan

A February 2026 profit miss and a two-month buyback pause tied to a legal circular; trading profits masked the weak plants. Sources: BOE Report: buyback pause · Shell news (Google)

Do now

Show profit per plant, per product, monthly

Stop hiding losses in averages.

This quarter

Sell or close the two worst sites

No credible fix, no future.

Big call

Buybacks only after the cash lands

Reward cash, not forecasts.

Crazy

Turn the sites into power hubs

Use land and grid links for data or energy.

Problem 03 · Strategy

Shell doubled back to oil — $16.5B for ARC Resources

$16.5B
ARC deal incl. debt, closed Sept 2026

Meanwhile it sold Sprng Energy and its European renewables portfolio to TotalEnergies and took FID on LNG Canada Phase 2. Sources: Shell news (Google)

Do now

One page per asset: cash return and emissions

Same numbers for investors and regulators.

This quarter

Stress every project at $60 oil

If it only works at $90, kill it.

Big call

Keep the best renewables, sell the rest

No trophy assets, no stranded costs.

Crazy

Spin out the transition unit as its own company

Let the market fund the pivot.
