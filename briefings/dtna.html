DTNA's market swings from a 39% sales drop to an 85% order surge while tariffs and charging gaps reshape the business.Pre-buy means customers ordering early to beat price hikes, which distorts demand. TCO is total cost to own and run a truck. The fix: strip noise from forecasts and track every truck in the field.

Problem 01 · Tariffs

A 25% truck tariff flipped sales from −39% to +85% orders

25%
US tariff on imported trucks

Profit halved by May 2026 on tariffs and weak demand; by August 2026 guidance was raised as buyers ordered ahead of price hikes. Sources: Reuters via Google News · Trucking Dive

This quarter

Price tariffs into every quote, weekly

Show exactly where electric wins.

This quarter

Big call

Own the bottleneck; sell the fuel.

Crazy
